Our Story

Discover the heritage and values behind MyClarks Kenya

Since 1825: A Legacy of Craftsmanship

For nearly 200 years, Clarks has been synonymous with quality, comfort, and style in footwear. Founded in Street, Somerset, England by brothers Cyrus and James Clark, our brand has stood the test of time through our commitment to exceptional craftsmanship.

In Kenya, we continue this legacy by bringing premium leather shoes that combine traditional shoemaking techniques with modern comfort technology. Our commitment to quality ensures that every pair of Clarks shoes delivers unmatched comfort and durability.
